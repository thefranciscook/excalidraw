## Fonts

The look-alike fonts bundled for the web-safe system fonts keep their own licenses, each included beside the font files in `packages/excalidraw/fonts/<family>/LICENSE.txt`:

- Tinos, Cousine, Gelasio, Comic Relief and Anton: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- DejaVu Sans: the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license. Its files here are subsets of the original font, split by script.
